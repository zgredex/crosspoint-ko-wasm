Engine: crosspoint-reader-ko 1.5.0-ko.3 (MIT) via WASM, KO typography build.
Reader font: RIDIBatang 14 (default, advanceY 38), KoPub Batang 14 (stock, advanceY 32), custom .epdfont via OTF/TTF conversion. Line heights are the firmware's, rounded: 38/46/53 px for RIDIBatang 14 and 32/38/45 px for KoPub 14 (Tight/Normal/Wide).
